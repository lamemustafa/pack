Try it

  • Click a recipe, or drag across cells to paint a range.
  • Click a year header to take that whole financial year — click two for two years. Click a row label for every available period of one return.
  • Scroll the matrix sideways for older years. Ten are available, matching the real getFiledReturnsFinancialYearOptions.
  • Press Build pack — July's GSTR-3B pauses for review, on purpose.

What it demonstrates

  • Time is one axis, grouped by year. A dropdown cannot express "the last three years", which is the whole point of a result-driven request.
  • Granularity belongs to the document. GSTR-9 is annual, so its row is one cell per year rather than twelve. An income-tax return would behave the same way.
  • Deliver and Also include are different questions. Individual files disables itself above five, because 56 files means 56 browser dialogs.
  • Computed artifacts are listed only when the selection could produce them, and say not built yet when Pack cannot yet make them. There is no consolidation code in src/ today, and the UI must not imply otherwise.
  • skipped is terminal and still blocks plan completion — the footer never says "complete" while a row is skipped.
