Matrix selects, rows track. Screen B is the selector — 27 targets in one gesture. Screen C is the ledger — a coloured cell cannot show an evidence class or a per-target next action, and a row can. Batch progress is an aggregate of rows and never replaces row truth: “26 of 27 saved, 1 skipped” is admissible; “complete” is admissible only when every target reaches a completion-eligible terminal state. Every cell is still one target with its own binding.

Marks — at the sizes that decide them

01 · The bound set

light toolbar
dark toolbar

Brackets closing around six marks — a set, enclosed. Four colours, nothing thinner than 1.7 units, so it resolves as one dense object at 16px. Recommended. The dark-toolbar row is the honest test: the navy field still needs a lighter variant, which is a field decision, not more detail.

02 · The filled year

dark toolbar

Periods filling in, last cell landing in ComplyEaze green. It means something — progress through a year. Three tonal fills are the first thing to muddy at 16px.

03 · The stamped P

dark toolbar

Safest at every size, least ownable. Close cousin of the Codex pass's Ledger P — the two passes agree on the criterion and differ on how far to travel from the current metaphor.
