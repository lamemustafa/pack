## Semantic decisions

- **Fixed plan versus growing eligibility:** retained PR1's recorded `targetPlan` and exact ordered
  matching. Rejected automatic plan growth and acceptance of incomplete v3 fixtures. Eligibility
  changes produce a warning; they do not redefine the run or authorize extra downloads.
- **Aggregate completion versus unresolved targets/ZIP:** retained catalogue's read-only
  inconsistency guard and extended recovery visibility to indexed plans. Rejected complete status
  winning over a target or cleanup guard. Inferred legacy staging must not hide an unresolved target.
- **Session summary versus durable recovery:** retained active-run and target-review precedence;
  discover indexed plans independently of session-summary scope. A newer unrelated summary cannot
  hide retained cleanup or unresolved targets. Canonical readers may re-save their redacted session
  projection; no new persisted field was introduced.
- **Malformed collection versus Clear:** protect individually valid unresolved records even when
  another record or the index is damaged. Rejected broad staging deletion that would erase their
  evidence. Explicit clearing of wholly settled malformed/orphan state remains available.
- **Replacement versus retained ownership:** replace only settled/cancelled same-scope records with
  no retained ZIP owner; preserve other scopes. An unstarted blocked plan follows recovery instead
  of being silently replaced. New record and index share one storage write, but old-record removal
  is separate. Failure deliberately leaves detectable malformed state; replacement is not claimed atomic.
- **Dynamic/legacy key cleanup:** remove only exact matching ID and scope; never erase an unrelated
  legacy record or adopt an unindexed ZIP owner.
- **Narrower-plan copy versus delivery truth:** coverage warning is supplementary. Catalogue's
  no-export, unconfirmed-download and blocking-recovery messages retain precedence; a marker alone
  cannot promote a blocked or single-period summary.
- **Registration versus rejection:** invariant 3 authorizes a bounded allowlist expansion for ten
  already-emitted fixed values: seven literal signals, PR1's invalid-plan reason, and two cleanup
  phase signals. This changes accepted values, not persisted fields. Rejected leaving known causes
  unregistered or accepting arbitrary suffixes/exception text.
- **Guided catalogue versus presets:** retained the catalogue lane's explicit four-step workflow.
  Preset-only date/price/refresh behavior is retired, not silently represented as still tested.
  Exact submitted scope, supported choices, artifact availability, recovery blocking and remedy
  visibility remain covered.
- **Hidden navigation versus route identity:** retained catalogue's canonical-auth-landing-only
  hidden-control predicate. Rejected PR1's broader tab-eligibility predicate: destination-origin
  checking after a click does not establish authority for the source-page hidden action. The
  summary-page fixture now asserts no click, the original off-landing negative is restored, and
  a caller test proves a fixed blocked reason before target work. Manual portal navigation is
  required for that refused path; automatic escape is deliberately deferred. Visible exact-anchor
  behavior, original-tab binding and download guards remain unchanged.
- **Conflicting fixtures:** incomplete-plan rejection now corrupts the recorded plan rather than
  eligibility metadata. The historical-complete rejection fixture is genuinely legacy, without
  v3 plan fields. Exact wrong-ID lookup reports not-found before recovery rather than returning
  another ledger for later mismatch rejection. Explicit retry expects the tab-pin callback.
  The provenance test resets leaked storage from the preceding case. None of these changes drops
  a case or weakens an assertion about actions, identity, or completion.
- **Review policy:** retained catalogue's stricter missing-review refusal. Its canonical default
  author remains enforced by the review-gate test; the former allow-missing switch was not restored.
  No PR/readiness claim or remote review operation is part of this task.
